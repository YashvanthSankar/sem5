Before configuring the tunnel, use the tracert command from one end device to the other and record the path followed by the packets. Then configure VPN between the routers connected to the two end LANs and route the required traffic through the tunnel. Verify successful communication between the end devices through the VPN. For Tunnel creation
#router0                    for present router

#tunnel destination 80.0.0.2            give the destination-router ip interface of the router
For Ip route
#ip route destination-ip subnetmask-of-tunnel-ip tunnel-ip  for IP route
