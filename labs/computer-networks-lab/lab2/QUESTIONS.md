## Q1 — Lowercase TCP client and server

Write `client.py` and `server.py` in Python. The server listens for a client,
receives text, converts it to lowercase, and sends the result back.

**Demonstrate both roles with a friend:**

1. Connect your client to your friend's server using their IP address and
   port. Print what you sent and received, plus both socket addresses
   (IP address and port).
2. Reverse roles: run your server and let your friend's client connect.
   Print the connecting client's socket address, received text, lowercase
   operation, and reply sent.

Put your name and roll number in comments at the start of both programs.
Take screenshots of both programs and their outputs. Combine screenshots,
notes, and your name/roll number on **every page** of one PDF. Submit that
PDF with both `.py` files individually before the deadline.

## Q2 — Network devices in Packet Tracer

Explore and demonstrate a **hub, switch, router, repeater, and bridge**.
Include a working ping and a Simulation-mode screenshot for the chosen
device scenarios.

Submit a PDF with comments and Packet Tracer screenshots; show your name and
roll number in Packet Tracer before capturing them. Submit a ZIP containing
the `.pkz` file(s), separately or combined. Submit individually before the
deadline.
